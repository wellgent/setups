## Planning workflow

Big or unclear efforts are charted with `wayfinder` as a map of decision tickets on this repo's GitHub Issues, and worked ticket by ticket with `/wayfinder <map link>`.
`grilling`, `domain-modeling`, `research` and `prototype` resolve the tickets, and `wizard` scripts the steps only a person can do.

- The tracker's operations and conventions are in `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`.
- The skills are pinned by commit in `skills-lock.json`. A pin moves on purpose, with `npx skills add`, and never with `skills update`.
